## Page 3: "3 Things to Fix" + Telemetry Comparison

**Header:** "Let's Fix These"

| Priority | Problem | Telemetry Evidence | Why It Matters | How To Fix It |
|----------|---------|-------------------|----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Braking too early | *"Your brake trace starts at 200m (blue line). Target starts at 150m (green line)."* | You lose speed before the corner | Move brake point 20m closer to the corner |
| 2 | Early apex at T9 | *"Steering angle increases after apex—see the red bump? That means you turned too early."* | You run wide on exit | Wait 1 car-length longer before turning |

## Page 5: Brake Analysis + Telemetry Reading

**Header:** "Your Braking - The Numbers Behind It"

**Graph:** Brake pressure trace (Your lap vs. Target lap)

**Annotations on the graph:**
- *"Blue drops here? That's your downshift blip."*
- *"Green line? That's the target. Notice the smooth decay."*

**Key Insights:**

| Observation | Telemetry Says | What It Means |
|-------------|----------------|---------------|
| Brake point too early | *"Your trace starts 50ft before target (blue starts left of green)."* | You're losing 0.25s before the corner even starts. |
| Brake release too abrupt | *"Your trace drops vertically. Target decays gradually (angled line)."* | You're snapping off brakes, which upsets the car. |
| Peak pressure too low | *"Your max: 80%. Target: 95%. That gap is lost stopping power."* | You're not using all the grip available. |

**Telemetry Lesson:** *"The brake trace is one of the most important charts. A smooth, angled drop is good. A vertical drop means you're not trail-braking."*

**Annotations:**
- *"Blue line (you) starts climbing later than green (target). That's lost time."*

**Key Insights:**

| Observation | Telemetry Says | What It Means |
|-------------|----------------|---------------|
| Too late on power | *"Throttle starts at 300ft after apex. Target starts at 250ft."* | You lose speed down the entire straight. |
| Abrupt application | *"Your throttle trace has a vertical wall. Target has a gentle curve."* | Spikes cause wheelspin. Squeeze it on. |
| Exit speed | *"92 mph vs. target 96 mph. That 4 mph gap shows in the speed trace."* | Lower exit speed = slower straight. |

**Telemetry Lesson:** *"The throttle trace should curve up like a hill, not climb like a staircase. Practice feeling the curve."*

## Page 7: Line Analysis + Telemetry Reading

**Header:** "Your Racing Line"

**Diagram:** Track map with lines (Your = red, Target = green)

**Annotations on map:**
- *"At T9, your red line touches the apex too early (early apex). Green line touches later."*
- *"At T1, red line leaves 3ft of track unused. Green uses all the road."*

**How to Read This:**

| What You See | What It Means | Telemetry Evidence |
|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| *Red line touches inside early* | Early apex. You'll run wide on exit. | Steering angle increases after apex (check Page 3). |
| *Red line leaves space at exit* | Not using all the track. | Speed at exit is lower than target. |
| *Red line matches green* | Perfect line. Don't change this. | Speed trace overlaps the target. |

**Telemetry Lesson:** *"The line determines your speed. Use all the track—outside, apex, outside. The telemetry shows when you don't."*

## Summary: The Balance

| Aspect | Beginner-First | Telemetry-Learning |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Language** | Plain English, analogies | Explains what each trace means |
| **Graphs** | Simple, annotated | Shows 2-lap overlay (Your vs. Target) |
| **Issues** | "You braked early" | "Here's the brake trace—see where blue starts before green?" |
| **Fixes** | "Try braking later" | "After your next session, check if the blue line moved right." |
| **Pages** | Starts with good news, ends with action | Each graph has a "What to look for" section |
| **Learning** | Reads like a coach | Gradually teaches telemetry literacy |

> *"The goal is not to make the driver feel bad. The goal is to make the driver faster AND smarter."* 🏁
