Here's the **tone guide** for APEX PDF reports aimed at **complete beginners**:

---

## The "Friendly Coach" Tone

### Core Principles

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Use **plain English** | Use racing jargon without explanation |
| Be **encouraging** | Be critical or harsh |
| Say **"you"** and **"your car"** | Say "the driver" or "the vehicle" |
| Explain **why** something matters | Just state the fact |
| Give **one clear fix** at a time | List 10 things to change |
| Use **analogies** from everyday driving | Assume track experience |

---

## Phrase Examples

### Instead of this (jargon-heavy)...
> *"Your trail-braking decay rate is suboptimal. You're exhibiting early apex syndrome at T3, resulting in excessive slip angle and compromised exit velocity."*

### Say this (beginner-friendly)...
> *"You're turning in too early at Turn 3. That means you run wide on exit and lose speed down the straight. Next time, wait just one car-length longer before turning the wheel."*

---

## The "Three-Bite" Rule

**Bite 1: What happened**
> "At Turn 1, you're braking at the 200m board."

**Bite 2: Why it matters**
> "That's earlier than needed. Braking early means you're losing speed before the corner even starts."

**Bite 3: How to fix it**
> "Try braking at the 150m board next session. Just 3-4 laps to get used to the feeling."

---

## Encouragement Language

| Situation | What to Say |
|-----------|-------------|
| **They did something right** | "Great job! You got on throttle early at T5—that's why you gained time down the straight." |
| **They made a mistake** | "This is really common. Most drivers do this. Here's how to fix it..." |
| **They're frustrated** | "Don't worry. Even pro drivers work on this exact corner. It takes practice." |
| **They improved** | "Look at that! You gained 0.3s just from that one change. Keep going!" |

---

## Action Plan Wording

**Instead of:**
> *"Adjust brake bias -2 clicks and increase rear wing angle."*

**Say:**
> *"The car is sliding on corner exit. Try moving the brake bias a little more to the rear (turn the knob toward 'rear' 2 clicks). This helps the rear tires grip better."*

---

## Final Rule: The "Grandma Test"

> *"Would your grandmother understand this?"*

If yes → keep it.  
If no → simplify it.

**Example:**
> *"Think of your tires like a balloon. If you squeeze too hard, they lose grip. Trail-braking is like slowly letting air out—smooth, gentle, controlled."*

---

## Quick Summary Checklist

- [ ] Short sentences (15 words max)
- [ ] No unexplained jargon
- [ ] One fix at a time
- [ ] Positive + encouraging
- [ ] Real-world analogies
- [ ] "You" + "your car"
- [ ] Celebrate small wins

> *"The goal is to make the driver feel like they just had a coaching session with a friend—not a lecture from an engineer."* 🏁
